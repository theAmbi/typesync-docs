# TypeSync — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 November 2026_

TypeSync ("the plugin") is a text-style manager for Adobe Photoshop, published by Golden Ekpendu.

## What TypeSync does with your data

**TypeSync does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data.**

- Your text styles, folder structure, layer links and settings are saved **only on your own computer**, in Photoshop's local plugin storage.
- TypeSync does not connect to the internet. It does not send any information to us or to any third party.
- TypeSync does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- Your documents are never read for any purpose other than showing and applying the typography of the text layers you choose in the panel.

## Data you create

Style names, folder names, and links between styles and layers are created by you and remain in local storage on your machine. Deleting the plugin, or resetting Photoshop's plugin data, removes them permanently. We have no access to them and cannot recover them.

## Changes

If a future version of TypeSync ever changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated and the change will be noted in the plugin's release notes before that version is released.
